After all the uncertainty, the teasing, the almosts, the ups and downs, and everything we had to go through just to find our way to each other… That little peck felt like the moment it was written in the stars.

Love has always been me vs. me constantly struggling to prove that I could do better, to give more, to become more, without ever expecting anything in return. Then you came along.

Weedor, you are more than just a woman I love. I’m grateful for every second I’ve spent with you the beautiful moments, the difficult ones, the laughter, the tears, and even the moments that tested us.
I’m forever grateful that I get to call you my partner. And if there is one thing I know for certain, it’s this: I will eternally cherish every moment, every memory, and every piece of love we’ve shared.🤍
